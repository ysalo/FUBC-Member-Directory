# Member Directory

## Language

**Family relationship**:
A connection between members through parenthood, marriage, explicitly established siblinghood, or kinship derived from recorded connections.

**Profile family**:
A member's parents, children, spouse, and siblings who are also members of the directory.
_Avoid_: Immediate family, when referring specifically to the profile display.

**Parent relationship**:
An explicitly established connection from one member to their child. Parenthood has no recorded subtype or fixed maximum number of parents; the inverse relationship is child.

**Spouse relationship**:
A mutual connection between two members who are currently married to each other. A member has at most one current spouse.

**Sibling relationship**:
A mutual connection explicitly established between two members or inferred from at least one shared recorded parent. An explicit sibling connection does not establish shared parents or relationships to additional siblings.
